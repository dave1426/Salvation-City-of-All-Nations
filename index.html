Identity

Who We Are

We are All Nations Prophetic Ministries, a Pentecostal Church. We believe in Jesus Christ as the Lord and Saviour and the Holy Bible as the authentic Word of God.

We believe and accept the lead of the Holy Spirit and the Demonstration of Power: Speaking in tongues and being baptized with Holy Spirit and Fire.

Our Great Commission is to win Souls for Christ and bring them into alignment with the Word of God. Changing the World and transforming lives through moral regeneration through the Holy Spirit and the Word of God.

Our Vision

We are an Antioch type of Church that is led by the Holy Spirit. We believe in Jesus Christ as our Lord and Saviour; and the Holy Bible as the authentic Word of God.

Win souls for Jesus Christ and disciple individuals.
Reach communities and cities by raising believers into disciples that mature into Ministers.

Commitment

Our Values

We believe that no matter where you are in your faith, there's always a next step you can take to better connect and grow in your relationship with God.

As a church, we are committed to continually growing and encouraging others to spend more time with God, follow Jesus, connect in community, serve others, and live more generously. This is who we want to become.
